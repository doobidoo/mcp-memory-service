- **The rerank stage measured on the negation pairs (#1674, #1697).**
  `scripts/benchmarks/benchmark_negation_rerank.py` compares plain cosine with the opt-in
  `rerank` flag on the 50 minimal pairs plus distractors from the resonance measurement:
  cosine scores 0.63 pairwise accuracy and reranking the top 40 lifts it to 0.87 (+24.0 p.p.,
  paired 95% CI [+14.0, +34.0], 27 queries improved and 3 regressed). Explicit negation goes
  0.88 → 1.00; implicit negation moves 0.32 → 0.48 and stays below chance, so the gap that
  motivated the resonance experiment is narrowed, not closed. The harness aborts if the stage
  degrades to fail-open and measures pool coverage on the pre-rerank ranking.
