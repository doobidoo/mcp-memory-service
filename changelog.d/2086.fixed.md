
 - **Natural language time search `between` ranges now preserve month years, accept `now`, and fall through on non-dates (#2086).**
      Queries with non-date words between `between` and `and` (such as "between Alice and Bob yesterday") returned `(None, None)` instead of falling through to match the relative date,
      failing time search queries with 400. In addition, explicit years following month names in the second position were truncated to bare month names and resolved to the current year,
      and `now` was rejected. `between` ranges now parse endpoints using `_parse_range_side` with sentence punctuation tolerance and fall through to single-expression parsers when
      endpoints are not valid times.
